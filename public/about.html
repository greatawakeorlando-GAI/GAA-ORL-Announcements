About Us

We are a Torah-based, Spirit-filled Awakened Assembly. We actively teach the Hebrew foundations of the faith.

We observe the weekly Sabbath (on the seventh day), known as Saturday, just as did the Savior, Luke 4:16, as well as Paul and the Apostles, Acts 13:42-46; 17:2; 18:4.

Singing, preaching, prayer, prophecy, special music and familiar songs are a part of our worship. Our membership comes from nearly all denominations and all walks of life.

We are NOT associated with any man-made denomination. We are a non-sectarian, non-denominational, non-camp affiliated community of Believers.

We are a peaceful, Holy Spirit-filled, prophetic community that will never use race bait tactics in the name of religion, use ethnically divisive theology, or use suggestive claims of racial superiority. We believe, like many, any believer of any nationality, ethnicity, creed, or tongue can know the Most High through His Son, Yahusha HaMashiach (Messiah), or as some call “Jesus.”

Our Mission

Our mission is to:

  1. Raise Awareness

    about how African Americans and other Bantus Negroes around the world are fulfilling biblical prophecy by rediscovering their ancient identity.

  2. Preach the Gospel

    to the lost tribes of Israel (Matt. 15:24; 10:5) and the nations.

  3. Awaken Israel Scattered

    all over the four corners of the earth (Isaiah 11:12).

  4. Build Bridges

    of relationship between affiliate Hebrew assemblies, outreach ministries, and organizations dedicated to awakening the dispersed of Israel in the communities they serve.

  5. Support Mission & Humanitarian Relief Initiatives

    where the dispersed are located, as a vehicle for the distribution of humanitarian goods and services to children and families.

What We Believe

Tap any belief to read it.

1

The Scriptures

We believe the Bible (the Tanakh and Apostolic Scriptures in their original autographs) is the inspired Word of God and our infallible guide in matters pertaining to conduct and doctrine (II Tim. 3:16; I Thess. 2:13; 2 Peter 1:21).

2

Holy Spirit

We believe there is one Elohim/God, Yahuah, eternally existent and revealed in His Son, Yahusha. The Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit) is the very spirit of Yahuah: the power, the character, the mind, the Nature, the Presence, the Teacher, and the Comforter that does the will of the Father, Yahuah.

3

Water Baptism

We believe in the mandate to be baptized (immersed) as a public act of ritual conversion from death to life, from the kingdom of darkness to the kingdom of light, from Satan’s domain to Yah’s domain (Matt. 28:18-20; Acts 2:37-38; Rom. 6:1-8).

4

Baptism of the Holy Spirit

We believe the Baptism in the Holy Spirit and fire is a gift from God as promised by Yahusha the Messiah to all believers, and is received subsequent to the New Birth (Matt. 3:11; John 14:16, 17; Acts 1:8; Acts 2:38, 39).

5

Yahusha the Messiah

We believe in Yahusha Ha-Mashiach as the only begotten Son of YAHUAH, in His virgin birth, in His sinless life, in His vicarious and atoning death through His shed blood, in His bodily resurrection, in His ascension to the right hand of the Father, and in His personal return in power and glory.

6

Israel

We believe in God’s everlasting covenant relationship with the Hebrew people, including the physical (Land of Israel) and the spiritual blessings (Genesis 12:1-3, 17:7; Jeremiah 31:31-37). In the end times, after tribulation, all of Israel will be saved (Zechariah 14; Romans 11).

7

The Gentiles

We believe Gentiles (non-Hebrews) who place their faith and trust in Yahusha the Messiah as Lord and Savior are grafted into Israel through a born again experience. As adopted Gentiles, they shall be afforded all the rights, privileges, and responsibilities of being full members of the commonwealth of Israel.

8

The Resurrection

We believe in the personal, physical return, in power and glory, of Yahusha the Messiah to judge the living and the dead (John 14:1-4; Acts 1:9-11; I Cor. 15:20-28; Hebrews 9:23-28). We believe in the physical resurrection of both the saved and the lost.

9

Sanctification

We believe that the Torah (five books of Moses) is a comprehensive summary of God’s foundational laws and ways, as found in both the Tanakh and Apostolic Scriptures. Without holiness no man can see God. We are set-apart by keeping God’s laws, statutes, and commandments.

10

Race / Identity

We reject the idea that race and ethnicity play a role in salvation. While the Assembly does teach that the true Israelites in the Bible were people of color, it has nothing to do with salvation by grace alone (Ephesians 2:8-9).
